# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Python Flask, HTML/CSS/JavaScript, and PostgreSQL

## Users

- Students submit complaints, upload images, track their complaints, and provide feedback.
- Lecturers report problems in classrooms, laboratories, offices, and equipment.
- Maintenance personnel receive assigned jobs, update progress, and resolve maintenance issues.
- Administrators manage users, complaints, assignments, reports, and system settings.

## Product Purpose

An online complaint and maintenance reporting system for University Of Professional Studies. It gives students and lecturers a way to report campus issues and follow their progress, while helping maintenance personnel and administrators coordinate resolution.

## Positioning

The system connects campus reporters, maintenance staff, and administrators through shared report status and job assignment, with a campus issue board that makes issue progress visible without exposing reporter contact details.

## Operating Context

Reports concern university spaces and assets, including classrooms, hostels, laboratories, offices, furniture, electrical systems, and equipment. Maintenance personnel work from assigned jobs and update their progress through resolution.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Reporters can submit maintenance complaints, attach images, track reports, and provide feedback.
- Lecturers can report issues in classrooms, laboratories, offices, and equipment.
- Maintenance personnel can receive assigned jobs, update progress, and resolve issues.
- Administrators can manage users, complaints, assignments, reports, and system settings.
- Administrators can control whether the issue board is public and whether anonymous reporting is enabled.
- A public campus issue board shows issue summaries, locations, categories, urgency, and status; reporter identity and contact information are not shown publicly.
- Report-form fields include location, category, urgency, description, photo, and an anonymous-report option.
- Authentication, university account integration, status vocabulary, and feedback timing remain undecided.

## Brand Commitments

The university name is University Of Professional Studies. No logo or other brand assets were supplied.

## Evidence on Hand

The supplied product brief describes campus maintenance and facilities complaints. No real university reports, logos, photographs, or other content assets were supplied; do not present invented reports or testimonials as real.

## Product Principles

- Make it easy for students and lecturers to report a specific campus issue.
- Keep each report's progress visible to its reporter.
- Give maintenance personnel clear assignment and resolution workflows.
- Protect reporter identity and contact details on the public issue board.
